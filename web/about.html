Campus navigation, built for IIT Jodhpur.

A student-built campus map

IITJ Maps is an interactive 3D map and navigation app for the IIT Jodhpur campus — search for buildings and landmarks, get walking and driving directions, and explore the campus in 3D. It’s a student project, made for the IITJ community.
