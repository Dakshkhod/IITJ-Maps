Usage and location data

IITJ Maps records an anonymous, tab-scoped session, approximate city or region, session duration, and routes calculated in the app. It does not store your IP address or attach this activity to an account or identity.

Precise location and movement are shared with IITJ Maps administrators only after you choose a location feature and accept the in-app notice. Telemetry is restricted to administrators and retained for up to 7 days.
