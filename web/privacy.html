Privacy

Campus navigation for IIT Jodhpur. Last updated June 2026.

No accounts · No tracking · No ads

Your location stays on your device

The map only asks for your location when you tap “locate me”, start navigation, or use “share my location”. When granted, your coordinates are used entirely in your browser to draw the blue dot and to compute routes. We do not send your location to any IITJ Maps server, and we do not store or log it. Close the tab and it’s gone.

What we store

  • Nothing about you on our servers. The app is a static site with no user database.
  • Your browser’s local storage may hold small preferences (e.g. last-used settings). These never leave your device.
  • No cookies are set for tracking. No analytics or advertising scripts run.

Third-party requests

Like any website, loading the map fetches resources from third parties — map tiles (CARTO, Esri), web fonts (Google Fonts, OpenMapTiles), and the MapLibre/Three.js libraries. Those providers necessarily see your IP address and the resource requested, governed by their own privacy policies. We pin these resources with Subresource Integrity so they can’t be tampered with in transit.

Permissions

The site requests only location (and motion sensors, for the compass) — and only on your action. Camera, microphone, and payment access are explicitly disabled at the browser level via Permissions-Policy.

Security

Traffic is HTTPS-only (HSTS). A strict Content-Security-Policy, frame protection, and other hardening headers are enforced. Administrative publishing is gated server-side; no privileged credentials are ever shipped to the browser.

Contact

Questions about this map or your data? Reach the IITJ Maps maintainer through IIT Jodhpur. We’ll update this page if practices change.
